# Service page illustration prompts

Built-in imagegen mode, transparent output. The input image for each prompt was a style reference only. Final PNGs were converted to WebP with alpha preserved.

## Debt Collection

Final asset: `public/assets/service-collection.webp`. Style reference: `public/assets/about-collection.webp`.

> Use case: illustration-story  
> Asset type: transparent editorial line illustration for the Debt Collection service-page hero on a financial services website.  
> Input image: supplied local illustration is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Do not repeat the two seated people, desk, speech bubble, or poses.  
> Primary request: one friendly Cloud adviser standing beside a large open account folder, gently organising a small stack of plain letters into three clear steps along a loose curved blue and orange path. The final step is a simple outlined check mark. Calm, orderly, considerate account handling; no distress or pressure.  
> Style/medium: same coherent cartoon family as reference: uniform bold near-black hand-drawn outlines, simple rounded character with white face, flat Cloud blue and warm orange accents, minimal shading.  
> Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, clear at medium website hero size, centered scene and generous transparent margins, no rectangular panel or background.  
> Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent.  
> Text: none.  
> Constraints: original composition, no other people, no large dashboard, no credit card, no phone, no money imagery, no logos, no numbers, no intimidating gestures, no gradients, no watermark.

## Legal Services

Final asset: `public/assets/service-legal.webp`. Style reference: the generated Debt Collection service artwork.

> Use case: illustration-story  
> Asset type: transparent editorial line illustration for the Legal Services page hero on a financial services website.  
> Input image: supplied local illustration is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Do not repeat its account folder sorting path, floating letters, check mark, character, or pose.  
> Primary request: a careful professional review of a case file: a single friendly adviser standing at a slim lectern, looking through one plain document with a magnifying glass.
