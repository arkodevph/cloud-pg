# About page illustration prompts

Built-in imagegen mode. Each referenced image supplied visual style only; all three final scenes are new. Transparent PNG results were converted to WebP without removing alpha.

## Team hero

Saved as `public/assets/about-team.webp`. Style reference: `public/assets/team-line.webp`.

> Use case: illustration-story  
> Asset type: transparent cutout illustration for the About Us hero on a Cloud Payment Group website.  
> Input image: the supplied local illustration is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY; do not repeat its dashboard, three-panel flow, or pose.  
> Primary request: make an entirely new, coherent editorial cartoon scene of a small professional team of three people collaborating at a rounded table. One adviser gestures warmly to a simple paper folder, a colleague listens, and a third looks at a plain tablet. Their body language conveys experienced, considerate customer service. A few tiny organic blue/orange accents and one loose curved connector can tie the group together, but keep the focus on the people.  
> Style/medium: match the reference's bold near-black hand-drawn outlines, simplified friendly faces, clean white fills, Cloud blue and warm orange clothing accents, restrained flat color, consistent character proportions and stroke weight.  
> Composition/framing: balanced wide 3:2 cutout; figures and table large enough to read inside a website hero panel, with comfortable transparent margins, no panel framing.  
> Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background.  
> Text: none.  
> Constraints: completely new composition and characters, no logos, no chart, no dashboard, no floating interface boxes, no gradients or photographic effects, no shadowed background, no watermarks.

## Payment management card

Saved as `public/assets/about-payment.webp`. Style reference: `public/assets/payment-line.webp`.

> Use case: illustration-story  
> Asset type: transparent cutout for the Payment Management service card on the About Us page.  
> Input image: the supplied local illustration is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Make a new scene, not its large phone with overlapping payment cards and standing character.  
> Primary request: a friendly customer sitting comfortably at a small desk with a laptop open to a simple account overview: three large, clear pictogram options (a card payment, a calendar arrangement, and a speech bubble for support), with no written labels or numbers. A tiny blue curved line connects the options. The scene communicates a choice of self-service payment paths and help, not a completed transaction.

> Composition/framing: readable at website card size; landscape 3:2 centered illustration with generous transparent margins. Make the person and laptop dominant, keep interface icons few and large.  
> Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent.  
> Text: none.  
> Constraints: fresh composition distinct from existing payment illustration; no giant phone, no floating credit cards, no charts, no logos, no fake account data, no gradients, no photographic effect, no watermark.
