# Google Flow hero film

The website currently has a static placeholder. Use the homepage hero as the composition reference: warm white background, a curved Cloud blue ribbon edged in coral orange, and four small illustrations in one thin black line-art style. Keep the headline, navigation, and buttons out of the generated film; they remain live HTML on the site.

## Frame 1 — normal size

> Wide 16:9 website hero visual on a clean warm-white background. One cyan-blue ribbon with a thin coral-orange edge bends gently in an S curve from the lower left toward the upper right. The ribbon carries subtle repeated Cloud Payment Group lettering. Four small, evenly spaced editorial line drawings stand along it: an account holder reading a letter, two people discussing options beside a clipboard, a person next to a phone with a checkmark, and a helpful support person beside a phone. All figures share exactly the same refined 2D style: thin slightly imperfect black pen lines, simple human proportions, mostly white fills, sparse cyan-blue and coral-orange accents. Two tiny white interface cards float near the ribbon. Plenty of empty white space above. Locked camera, no headline, no navigation, no photorealism, no 3D, no extra characters.

## Frame 2 — expanded card

> Exact same scene, camera, ribbon shape, people, colors, and line-art style as Frame 1. The small white payment-options card has moved forward and grown to occupy the center third of the visual, revealing three simple abstract option rows in Cloud blue and coral orange. The bent ribbon and four little figures remain visible behind it at normal scale. Clean warm-white background and empty space above remain unchanged. No headline, no navigation, no new elements, no style change, no morphing.

## Motion direction

> Begin at Frame 1. The payment-options card smoothly expands toward Frame 2, pauses briefly, then shrinks back to its exact original position and size. The ribbon bends with a slight gentle wave but retains the same overall shape. The four figures make only tiny natural movements. End on the same composition as Frame 1 for a seamless loop. One continuous locked-camera shot, 6–8 seconds, no cuts.

After export, the film can replace the static hero scene and be tied to scroll progress. The current page does not autoplay a missing video.
